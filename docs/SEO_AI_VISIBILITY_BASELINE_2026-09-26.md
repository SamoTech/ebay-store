## AI visibility measurement

Use docs/AI_VISIBILITY_TESTS.md for the repeatable query suite.

For every measurement, record:

1. Date.
2. Search system.
3. Query.
4. Whether Saleh Store was mentioned.
5. Exact Saleh Store URL cited.
6. Whether the cited page directly supported the answer.
7. Other sources cited.
8. Notes on missing, outdated, or weak information.

## Search Console acceptance criteria

For the next measurement cycle:

- Sitemap submitted and processed without errors.
- New guide URLs discovered.
- New guide URLs indexed where eligible.
- No accidental noindex or robots blocks on canonical content.
- Product and breadcrumb enhancements show no critical structured-data errors.
- Organic impressions begin accumulating for guide/category/product queries.

Do not interpret a page-one position as a guaranteed outcome. The objective is measurable growth in eligible, useful, cited, and organically discovered content.
