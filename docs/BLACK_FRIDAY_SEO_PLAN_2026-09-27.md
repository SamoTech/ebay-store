## Objective

Build the Black Friday guide around a defensible search-intent niche instead of attempting to rank for the generic term "Black Friday 2026" alone.

A first-page ranking is not guaranteed. This plan prioritizes relevance, crawlable internal linking, useful content, and query-specific coverage.

## Why this niche

The generic Black Friday SERP is dominated by major retailers and large editorial/deals publishers. Current 2026 results also show dedicated pages for gaming laptops, tech-category guides, and price-history methodologies.

eBay's own Black Friday page organizes its event around electronics, laptops, smartphones, TVs/home audio, video games/consoles, home/garden, fashion, sports, and automotive. This makes "eBay + Black Friday + category" a natural query architecture for an eBay-focused shopping site.

## Evidence notes

Google Search Central recommends descriptive, concise titles, avoiding keyword stuffing, and using meaningful internal-link anchor text. Ecommerce guidance also notes that internal link structure helps Google understand page relationships and relative importance.

eBay's current US Black Friday page groups deals across Electronics, Laptops, Smartphones, TV/Home Audio, Video Games/Consoles, Home & Garden, Fashion, Sports, Automotive, and more.

Current 2026 search results show active coverage around Black Friday gaming laptops, tech deals, and price-history verification, confirming that category and problem-intent subtopics are real search themes rather than invented keyword combinations.
